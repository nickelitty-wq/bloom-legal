I am App Alternatives: 3 Options Worth Trying

Updated November 2026

I am is the best-known affirmation app, and for a lot of people it works well. Common reasons to look elsewhere are the price, wanting affirmations that fit your beliefs, or wanting a different way to practice. Here are the alternatives and who each suits.

We make Bloom, so weigh our opinion accordingly. We have tried to be fair and to say when another app is the better choice.

1. Bloom: personalized, in your style of wording

Bloom works like I am in that affirmations come to you as reminders, but it starts with a short setup about what you are working on and builds your mix from 1,500+ affirmations across 10 categories. You also choose secular, spiritual or faith-based wording. At US list prices it is $39.99 a year versus $69.99.

Choose it if: you liked I am's reminder style but want it more personal, or cheaper. Skip it if: you need Android.

2. ThinkUp: hear affirmations in your own voice

ThinkUp has you record affirmations yourself and play them back with music. It is a different practice from reading reminders, and some people find it sticks better.

Choose it if: you prefer listening, or want to write fully custom affirmations.

3. The free option: Notes and Reminders

If cost is the main issue, write 10 affirmations that feel true in your Notes app and set one daily reminder to read them. You lose variety and personalization, but the core habit is the same.

Questions

What is a good alternative to the I am app?

Bloom is the closest alternative if you like reminder-style affirmations but want them personalized, with secular, spiritual or faith-based wording and a lower yearly price. ThinkUp is the better alternative if you want to record affirmations in your own voice.

Is there an app like I am but cheaper?

At US list prices checked in November 2026, Bloom and ThinkUp are both $39.99 per year, compared with $69.99 for I am. Prices and offers change, so check the App Store before subscribing.

Can I move my favorites from I am to another app?

There is no automatic transfer between affirmation apps. The simplest approach is to copy your favorite lines into a note, then save the matching ones in your new app.
