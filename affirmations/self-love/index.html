Self-Love Affirmations

Most of us talk to ourselves in a way we would never talk to a friend. Self-love affirmations are practice for a kinder inner voice. These lines are about accepting where you are, giving yourself the patience you give others, and letting your worth stay steady on hard days.

How to use them

Say these to yourself in the mirror or write one at the top of a journal page. If self-love feels like a stretch, start with neutral lines like 'I am learning to be patient with myself'.
