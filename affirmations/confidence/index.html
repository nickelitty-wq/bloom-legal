30 Confidence Affirmations for When You Feel Unsure

Confidence is rarely a feeling you wait for. It usually comes from showing up while you are still unsure. These affirmations focus on small, believable statements about courage, progress and owning your strengths, so they still land on days you don't feel confident at all.

How to use them

Read one before the moment that makes you nervous: a meeting, an interview, a hard conversation. Choose the line that feels most true, not the one that sounds the boldest.
