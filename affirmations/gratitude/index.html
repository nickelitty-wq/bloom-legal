30 Gratitude Affirmations for Noticing the Good

Gratitude is less about big moments and more about noticing small ones. These affirmations point your attention at what is already working: people, comforts, progress and simple joys that are easy to miss in a busy day.

30 gratitude affirmations

  2. I am grateful to be alive, right now.
  3. I sense appreciation changes how I see my life.
  4. I give thanks for what is going right.
  5. I find gratitude even on hard days.
  6. I know gratitude shifts my focus toward the good.

  9. I feel rich in what I already have.

  17. I know gratitude makes the present feel like enough.
  18. I am grateful for another chance to begin.
  19. I give thanks for lessons in my struggles.
  20. I appreciate the simple gift of being here.
  21. I sense thankfulness fills the space worry left.
  22. I am grateful for people who feel like home.
  23. I notice the good, even when small.
  24. I give thanks for my own resilience.

  26. I am thankful for comforts I rush past.
  27. I appreciate the reliable things I depend on.
  28. I savor a moment of joy fully.
  29. I am grateful for laughter, rest, and warmth.
  30. I name three good things today.

How to use them

Read one in the evening and add one specific thing from your day that it reminds you of. Specific beats general.
