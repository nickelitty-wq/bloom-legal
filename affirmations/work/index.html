18 Affirmations for Work and Career Confidence

Work brings its own kind of pressure: proving yourself, asking for what you are worth, staying steady when things get busy. These affirmations are for the commute, the minute before a meeting or the end of a long day.

  1. I trust money comes to me through honest work.
  2. I sense my work proves more than my doubt.
  3. I stand on the work I have already done.
  4. I am a magnet for fair, meaningful work.
  5. I charge what my work is worth.
  6. I welcome clients who value my work.
  7. I welcome the wealth good work creates.
  8. I let my work matter, even in small ways.
  9. I believe my work expresses my deepest values.
  10. I trust meaningful work takes time to build.
  11. I trust meaning lives in the work I do today.
  12. I trust my work is a contribution, not just a task.
  13. I understand my work leaves a mark that lasts.
  14. I trust my work aligns with who I am becoming.
  15. I understand my work is part of something larger.
  16. I give my purpose the work it takes.
  17. I know my work has value before it is recognized.
  18. I negotiate for what my work is worth.

How to use them

Keep one on a sticky note by your screen or set it as your lock screen for the week. Change it when your focus at work changes.
