Questions

How do I use spiritual affirmations?

Read one slowly at the start or end of the day, ideally with a few deep breaths. If a line feels like a stretch, keep it anyway for a week and notice whether it starts to feel truer.

How often should I repeat affirmations?

Daily is more useful than long sessions. One or two affirmations in the morning, and one you come back to when you need it, is enough to build the habit.
