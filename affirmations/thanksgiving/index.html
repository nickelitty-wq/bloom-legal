30 Thanksgiving Gratitude Affirmations (Plus 10 Table Prompts)

Thanksgiving is a built-in reminder to notice what is going right, but the day itself can be busy and loud. These gratitude affirmations are for the quiet moments around it, and the prompts below are for turning gratitude into a conversation at the table.

  1. I give thanks for the love already here.
  2. I appreciate my body, my life, and my mind.
  3. I am thankful for the chance to grow.
  4. I am grateful for unasked-for support.
  5. I give thanks for the path that brought me here.
  6. I appreciate the people who believe in me.
  7. I trust thankfulness widens my view of life.
  8. I am grateful for small wins that add up.
  9. I give thanks for the easy and the hard.
  10. I am thankful for a fresh start each morning.
  11. I appreciate the blessings I often miss.
  12. I feel grateful before I ask for more.
  13. I am grateful for familiar comforts.
  14. I give thanks for the strength I found.
  15. I am thankful for home, within and around me.
  16. I appreciate the gift of another day.
  17. I give thanks for people who show up.
  18. I am grateful for nourishment, rest, and care.
  19. I appreciate the lessons my mistakes taught.
  20. I feel stable through thankfulness.
  21. I am grateful for love given and received.
  22. I give thanks for my imperfect health.
  23. I am thankful for moments that make me smile.
  24. I appreciate how quietly life supports me.
  25. I am grateful for the chance to try again.
  26. I give thanks for staying alive.
  27. I feel appreciation deepens my joy.
  28. I am thankful for people who feel safe.
  29. I appreciate the beauty in this moment.
  30. I am grateful for growth hidden in struggle.

10 gratitude prompts for the table

  1. Who made this year a little easier for you, even in a small way?
  2. What is something ordinary you would miss if it disappeared tomorrow?
  3. What hard moment from this year taught you something?
  4. Which dish or tradition on this table means the most to you, and why?
  5. Who here would you thank for something they probably don't remember doing?
  6. What is one thing about yourself you are grateful for this year?
  7. Where felt most like home this year?
  8. What made you laugh the hardest this year?
  9. What is a habit or skill you are glad you built?
  10. What are you looking forward to next year?

How to use them

Read one in the morning before the day gets busy. At dinner, pick two or three prompts below and go around the table.
