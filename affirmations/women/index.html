40 Affirmations for Women: Self-Worth, Confidence, Boundaries and Rest

Most lists of affirmations for women circle the same few themes: feeling like enough, speaking up, saying no, making peace with your body and resting without guilt. This list covers those five, eight affirmations each, chosen from Bloom's library of 1,500+ affirmations. They aren't written only for women, and anyone can use them.

Self-worth

Worth that doesn't depend on a good day, a compliment or a finished to-do list.

  1. I am enough exactly as I am right now.
  2. I keep my worth constant, not conditional.
  3. I have worth that existed before today's achievements.
  4. I stop auditioning for worth I already have.
  5. I don't need fixing to be lovable.
  6. I choose to be on my own side.
  7. I am kind to the person in the mirror.
  8. I treat myself like someone worth encouraging.

Confidence and speaking up

For meetings, negotiations and any room where you're tempted to make yourself smaller.

  1. I take up space without explaining myself.
  2. I trust my voice, even when it shakes.
  3. I voice my opinion; my perspective has value.
  4. I stop asking permission to take up space.
  5. I am qualified enough to start.
  6. I am capable of more than my comfort admits.
  7. I can ask for fair compensation.
  8. I trust the strength I have quietly built.

Boundaries

Saying no, giving what you can realistically give, and keeping the people who don't need you small.

  1. I no longer shrink to keep others comfortable.
  2. I choose myself without calling it selfish.
  3. I protect my energy without explaining why.
  4. I release the apology that wasn't mine to give.
  5. I tell people what I can realistically give.
  6. I release relationships that need me small.
  7. I choose connection that does not require shrinking.
  8. I am worthy of the effort I give others.

Body image

A kinder relationship with your body through every season and change.

  1. I am more than how my body looks today.
  2. I am kind to my body in every phase of life.
  3. I release comparing my body to others.
  4. I feel my body is my partner, not my opponent.
  5. I move to honor my body, not earn worth.
  6. I choose care over criticism in the mirror.
  7. I am kind to my body through every change.
  8. I thank my body instead of criticizing it.

Rest without guilt

For when you look after everyone else first and call it normal.

  1. I release guilt for putting myself first.
  2. I treat self-care as maintenance, not luxury.
  3. I receive the care I freely give.
  4. I give myself the pause I have been denying.
  5. I deserve slow mornings and relaxed evenings.
  6. I give myself the grace I give everyone else.
  7. I trust rest is a healthy choice, not laziness.
  8. I stop waiting for permission to be kind to myself.

How to use them

Pick the section that fits this season of your life and choose one line from it. Say it in the morning and come back to it at the moment it's for: before the meeting, after saying no, in front of the mirror.
