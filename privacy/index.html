Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Bloom Affirmations respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app and our website, bloomaffirmations.app.

01 Information Stored on Your Device

Bloom does not ask for your email address or create an account. Most app data is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Affirmation preferences and favorites
  • Streak and usage data
  • Notification and widget settings
  • Onboarding and personalization responses, such as your first name, mood, relationship status, employment status, zodiac sign, gender, theme preference, and spiritual/religious preference

This data is used to tailor the affirmation content and app experience shown to you. Some onboarding answers are also included in analytics, as described in section 02.

02 Analytics

Bloom uses PostHog to understand how the app is used and to improve the product. PostHog collects usage and event data linked to a random app identifier, not to your name or contact details. This includes:

  • Which screens and features you use, such as onboarding steps, the paywall, focus sessions, favorites, and opening the app from the widget
  • Some onboarding answers: your mood, what motivates you, your main focus area, and how you heard about Bloom
  • When you share an affirmation: which app you shared to (for example Messages or WhatsApp), whether it succeeded, and the affirmation's category. We do not see who you shared with or any message content.
  • Purchase and subscription events, such as viewing the paywall or starting a purchase
  • Basic device and app information, such as app version and device type

Your first name, relationship status, employment status, zodiac sign, gender, and religious preference are not sent to analytics. Analytics data is reviewed in aggregate and is not sold. Review PostHog's privacy policy at posthog.com/privacy.

03 Home Screen and Lock Screen Widget

If you add the Bloom widget, the app saves a set of affirmations, your theme color, and your refresh setting to storage shared between the app and the widget on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

04 Sharing

When you choose to share an affirmation or share Bloom, the app opens your device's share sheet or the app you pick. What you send, and who you send it to, is handled by that app and is never sent to us. Shared messages include a link to Bloom on the App Store. Apple may report how many people installed Bloom through that link, in aggregate, without identifying anyone.

05 Subscription and Payment Data

Bloom uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat and Apple may collect certain information required to process payments, such as purchase history, device identifiers, and subscription status.

Bloom does not have access to your full payment details. All transactions are handled securely through the App Store. Review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

06 Permissions

Bloom may request permission to send notifications to deliver your affirmation reminders. You can manage this permission in your device settings at any time.

07 Our Website

bloomaffirmations.app does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them (see Google's privacy policy). The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to serve and protect the site. Links to the App Store include a campaign tag so Apple can tell us, in aggregate, how many installs came from the website.

08 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Most app data remains on your device; analytics data sent to PostHog is transmitted securely for the purposes described above.

09 Your Rights and Choices

You can change your preferences in the app's settings, and deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your device, email us. For subscription-related data, manage your information through your Apple ID account settings.

10 Children's Privacy

Bloom is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
