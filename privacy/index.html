Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Bloom — Daily Affirmations respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

01 Information We Collect

Bloom does not collect personally identifiable information directly. Most app data is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Affirmation preferences and favorites
  • Streak and usage data
  • Notification settings
  • Onboarding responses

02 Subscription and Payment Data

Bloom uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat and Apple may collect certain information required to process payments, such as purchase history, device identifiers, and subscription status.

Bloom does not have access to your full payment details. All transactions are handled securely through the App Store. Review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

03 Permissions

Bloom may request permission to send notifications to deliver your daily affirmation reminders. These permissions can be managed in your device settings at any time.

04 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Most app data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

05 Your Rights and Choices

You may control, update, or delete your locally stored data by managing app settings or uninstalling the app. For subscription-related data, manage your information through your Apple ID account settings.

06 Children's Privacy

Bloom is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
