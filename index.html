Questions

What is Bloom?

Bloom is an iPhone app for daily positive affirmations. It has 1,500+ affirmations across 10 categories, including anxiety and calm, confidence, self-love, gratitude and abundance, and it sends them as reminders through your day.

How much does Bloom cost?

Bloom is free to download. Full access comes with Bloom Premium at $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year (US pricing; your App Store shows local prices). Eligible new subscribers may see an introductory price for the first year.

Is Bloom religious?

Only if you want it to be. During setup you choose secular, spiritual or faith-based wording, and every affirmation is shown in that style.

Is Bloom on Android?

Not yet. Bloom is currently available on iPhone.

Do affirmations actually work?

Affirmations are not magic, but repeating short, believable statements can help shift how you talk to yourself over time, especially when you practice daily. Bloom is built around making that daily habit easy.
