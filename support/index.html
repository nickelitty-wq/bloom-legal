Common questions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Bloom. In recent versions of Bloom you can also go to the Settings tab and tap manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple ID, choose the Bloom purchase and select Request a refund. Apple decides on refunds; if you are unsure what you were charged for, email us and we will help you figure it out.

I paid but Bloom still shows the paywall. What do I do?

Open the Settings tab in Bloom and tap restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I change or turn off reminders?

In Bloom, open the Settings tab and tap daily reminders to choose how many reminders you get and when. To turn them off completely, open iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Bloom.

How do I add the Bloom widget?

Home screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Bloom and pick a size. Lock screen: touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen and add Bloom. You can choose how often it shows a new affirmation in the Settings tab under widget.

Where is my data stored?

Bloom does not create an account or ask for your email. Your preferences, favorites and streak are stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy for the analytics we use and what is never sent.
